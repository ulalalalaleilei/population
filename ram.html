Historical Overview & Growth Milestones

Baseline (1950)
~2.5 Billion

The estimated baseline population scale around the mid-twentieth century (1950).

Milestone (1987)
5 Billion

Human numbers doubled in approximately 37 years from 1950, surpassing 5 billion in 1987.

Peak Growth (1962–65)
2.1% / year

The fastest relative growth rate recorded during the century, increasing by 2.1% annually.

Overall Growth
3× Larger

The world's population is now more than three times larger than it was in mid-1950.

Trend Analysis: While global numbers continue to rise, the rate of population growth has slowed significantly since its peak in the 1960s.

The death rate or the number of deaths in a given population.

Slide 4 of 6 • Regional Dynamics

Regional Dynamics & Changing Rankings

Eastern & South-Eastern Asia

Held the largest regional population in the early 2020s (over 2.3 billion). China and India each accounted for more than 1.4 billion people, shaping global demographic weights.

India & China Ranking Shift

In 2023, India officially surpassed China to become the world's most populous country, marking a historic turning point in international population rankings.

Sub-Saharan Africa Growth

The fastest-growing region with a 2.5% annual growth rate in 2022. Its population is projected to nearly double and surpass 2 billion inhabitants by the late 2040s.

Slide 6 of 6 • Future Projections to 2050

Global Milestone & 8 Growth-Driving Countries

Global Milestone (2050)

United Nations projections estimate that the global population will reach approximately 9.7 billion by 2050, with overall annual growth rates falling below 1 percent.

More than half of global population growth up to 2050 will be concentrated in just eight key nations.
